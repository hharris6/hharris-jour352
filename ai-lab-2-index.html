City Council Approves New Bike Lanes on Main Street
By Jordan Ellis | Oct. 26, 2026
A cyclist rides along Main Street on Monday. (Photo by Sam Rivera)

The City Council voted 5-2 on Monday to add protected bike lanes along a mile-long stretch of Main Street, a project supporters say will make the downtown corridor safer for cyclists and pedestrians.

Construction is expected to begin in the spring and cost about $1.2 million, according to the city's transportation department.

"This is about making sure everyone can get downtown safely, whether they drive, walk or bike."

Opponents argued the lanes would remove about 40 parking spaces used by local businesses.
